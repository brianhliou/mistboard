# Sponsorship

Mistboard may accept sponsorships for tournaments, engine events, research artifacts, infrastructure, and community work.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, sponsorships are commercial support, not tax-deductible charitable donations.

For broader monetization and project-direction boundaries, see `docs/project-direction.md`.

## Principles

- Sponsors do not buy roadmap control.
- Sponsored work should be disclosed.
- The project may reject sponsors that create brand, legal, community, or integrity risk.
- Tournament and prize-pool funds should use a clean payment rail, such as a fiscal host or later a project-owned company account.
- Personal-wallet donations should be avoided for public project funding.

## What Sponsors May Fund

Examples:

- tournament prize pools
- broadcast/replay production costs
- engine benchmark reports
- public research writeups
- infrastructure costs
- specific open-source improvements
- community events

## What Sponsors Receive

Possible benefits, depending on the event or agreement:

- logo placement on event pages
- replay or broadcast attribution
- naming sponsorship for a specific tournament
- public thank-you notes
- inclusion in a post-event report
- optional technical writeup collaboration for research-aligned sponsors

## What Sponsors Do Not Receive

Sponsors do not receive:

- control over the roadmap
- control over tournament results, pairings, or benchmarks
- access to hidden production data
- private player information
- commit or release authority
- trademark ownership
- exclusivity unless explicitly documented

## Fiscal Hosting

If Mistboard uses Open Collective or another fiscal host, that host is a payment and administration layer for defined funds. It does not own Mistboard IP, domains, trademarks, production infrastructure, package names, or product direction.

## Transparency

For meaningful sponsorships, Mistboard should publish:

- sponsor name
- sponsored event or work
- what was funded
- any sponsor benefits or placement
- final tournament/prize-pool accounting when relevant

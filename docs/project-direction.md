# Project direction

## Mission

**Bring people together across cultures through Chinese chess and the world's
classic strategy games.**

Chinese chess is the flagship: xiangqi and its traditional relatives, Jieqi and
Banqi, in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. The world's other
classic strategy games sit beside it, along with a few we design on a xiangqi
board.

## What we build

- **Play:** link games with no account, a lobby, rated play, correspondence and
  computer opponents.
- **Study:** puzzles, courses, studies, analysis and a games database.
- **Watch:** live games, tournament broadcasts and replays.
- **Engines:** public title matches and the [engine protocol](engine-protocol.md).

## How we decide

Each change answers one question: does it make Mistboard a more trustworthy
place to play, study, rank, or build engines for Chinese chess and its
relatives? Ratings, matchmaking, chat, moderation, payments and sign-in carry
integrity and support costs, so work on them starts with an issue.

The player-facing bots are named by engine and level (Pikafish, Fairy-Stockfish,
Misty and others). Version strings stay in replay data for reproducibility.

## Reference

Use compatible open-source libraries (GPL-3.0, MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD and similar),
public standards and original work, and nothing with unclear rights. Do not copy
source, layouts, assets, copy, lessons, puzzles or databases from other game
platforms. Name, forks and sponsorship are covered in
[GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md).

# Governance

Mistboard is run by its founder, Brian H. Liou, who makes the final call on
the product, the roadmap and what is merged.

## Open and controlled

The code is AGPL-3.0-or-later: anyone may use, change and share it, and anyone
who runs a changed version as a network service must offer its source to their
users. The Mistboard name and logo, the domains, the hosted service, its data
and infrastructure, and Mistboard events stay with the project. Commit access is
granted by the maintainer.

## Name and forks

You may use the name to refer to Mistboard, link to it, or say your work is
built on or compatible with it. Do not imply that your site, fork or event is
official or endorsed. Forks are welcome under their own name.

## Sponsors and patrons

Sponsors and patrons get no say over the roadmap, game results, ratings or
data. Sponsored work is disclosed. Patron support is described at
[mistboard.com/patron](https://mistboard.com/patron).

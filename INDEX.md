| `xiangqi-exchange.ts` | Static exchange evaluation for standard xiangqi (`xiangqiStaticExchange`, swap-list SEE over elephantops attack sets, cannon screens recomputed per capture), the pieces a side can take at a profit (`xiangqiEnPrisePieces`, opening capture optionally restricted to LEGAL moves), the one-ply material story of a move (`xiangqiMoveMaterial`: captured, offered net of prior exposure), and `xiangqiSettleAlongLine` (material where an engine line goes quiet; checks keep it forcing). Deliberately never reads a later GAME ply: the three material-trajectory detectors that did are the fixture `fixtures/xiangqi-move-classification/known-cases.json` |
| `xiangqi-move-classification.ts` | Positive move glyphs for xiangqi review: `classifyXiangqiMove` -> `brilliant` (`!!`: a piece offered, the engine's PV or capture line leaves the mover materially down or mates, mover not worse after, not winning anyway per the second-best line) or `great` (`!`: engine's top choice, second-best >= 10 win% worse, punishes an opponent error >= 5, not a free capture/recapture/rescue, mover >= 50% after). Rules follow chess.com's published Game Review definitions and the freechess/en-croissant clones (research: docs-private/xiangqi-positive-annotations-research.md, issue #314). Both glyphs fire in production. `great` needs the runner-up move, and the server sweep runs MultiPV 2 for exactly that reason (`XIANGQI_ANALYSIS_MULTI_PV`, #315) — both lines leave ONE search, so their gap is a comparison rather than search noise; `brilliant` needs the PV after the move, also persisted. `tree-review.ts` (`praiseByPly`) supplies `secondBestWin` from `PlyEval.second` and `winTwoPliesAgo` from the eval two plies back. The remaining gap is rows cached before MultiPV 2 (analysis engine version <= 4), which carry no `second`: on those games `!` is silently impossible — no error, just an absent glyph. Fixtures: `known-cases.json` (must NOT mark), `positive-cases.json` (must mark, with the engine numbers) |

## Conventions

- **TypeScript everywhere.** No `.mjs` in source; ops scripts in `scripts/` can be `.mjs`.
- **Pure-game in `packages/game`.** No browser or server imports allowed.
- **Server owns canonical state.** Clients render `PlayerView`, never canonical truth.
- **Fog redaction in `payloads.ts`.** Never send hidden pieces or opponent moves to the wrong client.
- **One source for time controls** → `packages/game/src/time-controls.ts`.
- **`.env` files are off-limits** to Claude — touching them leaks via auto-include. Use Node `--env-file` or provider dashboards.
- **Lesson trailers** on commits that teach a transferable rule (see `~/projects/CLAUDE.md`).

# CC0 xiangqi network

`xiangqi-c07e94a5c7cb.nnue` is the Fairy-Stockfish xiangqi network published
under CC0. It is distributed separately from the GPL-3.0-or-later engine.

- Network source: [Fairy-Stockfish-NNUE at the pinned commit](https://github.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish-NNUE/tree/0f4dce21530775b1dc862b7c84aa707548183097)
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Exact download URL, commit, byte size and SHA-256: `net.json`
- Who says it is CC0: the network's authors, the Pikafish developers, in the
  [Pikafish Networks README](https://github.com/official-pikafish/Networks/blob/master/README.md):
  the weights they train for the xiangqi variant of Fairy-Stockfish are licensed
  under CC0 and are not constrained by the Pikafish weights license. The
  Fairy-Stockfish-NNUE repository that hosts the file carries the engine's GPL
  text and no separate statement for the network.
